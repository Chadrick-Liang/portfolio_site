Fake Job Detector

A deep learning classifier built around a difficult imbalance: just 866 fraudulent postings among 17,880 examples.

Custom fastText embeddings, a bidirectional GRU, and multi-head attention extract patterns from text. A second branch adds structured job attributes, while focal loss addresses the class imbalance.

The best recorded test result reached an F1-score of 0.90 for fraudulent postings, with 0.95 precision and 0.86 recall.
