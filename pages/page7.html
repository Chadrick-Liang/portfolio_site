IM-CONNECTED

A team-built web app designed to ease the day-to-day workload of caregivers.

A multilingual forum offers a place to share experiences among other caregivers. An OCR scanner reads medication labels for identification and logging, while an AI chatbot remembers conversations, provides non-judgemental support and summarises forum posts.

  • React
  • Express
  • MongoDB
  • Tesseract.js
